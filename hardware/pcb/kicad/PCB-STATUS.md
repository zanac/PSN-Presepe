# PCB CAD status — Rev A

`PSN-Presepe-Mega.kicad_pcb` is the first physical board source.

Current content:
- 220 x 120 mm working outline;
- 1.6 mm / 2-layer board setup;
- placement zones for Mega, SELV drivers, relays, relay terminals and low-voltage terminals;
- explicit isolation-barrier marker;
- five 3.2 mm mechanical mounting holes;
- fabrication warning on silkscreen.

The rectangles are **placement guides**, not electrical footprints.

No Mega header pads or G5Q pads have been guessed. Their actual footprints must be created from manufacturer mechanical drawings/CAD before electrical placement and routing.

No Gerber generated from this revision is suitable for fabrication.
