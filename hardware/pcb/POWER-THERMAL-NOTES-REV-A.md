# Power and thermal notes — Rev A

## 12 V input
Rev A has one external 12 V input. The same 12 V rail supplies:
- Arduino Mega through VIN;
- 16 relay coils;
- external 12 V RGB loads through their common-positive terminals;
- STELLE and CASETTE 12 V outputs.

The 5 V rail is generated by the Mega and is reserved for low-current logic/peripherals such as OLED and B10K.

## Arduino Mega VIN thermal note
The official Arduino Mega 2560 Rev3 datasheet specifies VIN/DC-jack recommended operating range up to 12 V. Rev A therefore stays inside the stated voltage range at a nominal 12 V input.

However, the Mega uses an onboard 5 V regulator. Supplying 12 V through VIN means the regulator must dissipate approximately:

`P = (VIN - 5 V) × I_5V`

Examples, excluding the Mega's own internal consumption:
- extra 20 mA on 5 V → about 0.14 W additional regulator dissipation;
- extra 50 mA → about 0.35 W;
- extra 100 mA → about 0.70 W.

Therefore do not use +5V_MEGA as a general-purpose high-current supply. Final assembly should verify regulator temperature under the real OLED/peripheral load.

## Relay-coil budget
For the intended Omron G5Q-1 DC12 SPDT type, the manufacturer data gives approximately 33.3 mA / 400 mW per 12 V coil.

Worst case with all 16 relays energized:
- coil current ≈ 16 × 33.3 mA = **0.533 A**;
- coil power ≈ **6.4 W**.

This is only the relay-coil load and does not include Mega, RGB strips, WS2811 strings or other 12 V loads.

The +12 V PCB distribution and connector/current rating must therefore be sized from the actual maximum simultaneous load, not merely from the few watts switched by the relay contacts.

## ULN2803
Each ULN2803 drives eight relay coils. With all eight corresponding G5Q-1 DC12 coils active, each package sinks roughly 266 mA total before considering tolerances.

Final validation must check:
- actual ULN2803 variant/datasheet;
- package dissipation at the measured coil current and saturation voltage;
- ambient/enclosure temperature;
- copper and ventilation around U1/U2.

## MOSFETs
Nine IRLZ44N TO-220 devices switch the negative RGB channels. Keep the reserved heatsink/airflow space.

The metal tab is drain-connected. Separate channel heatsinks must not electrically touch one another unless electrically insulated.

## Input supply sizing
Do not choose the 12 V supply from relay current alone. Add:
1. worst-case RGB-strip current;
2. WS2811 STELLE current;
3. WS2811 CASETTE current;
4. relay-coil worst case;
5. Mega/logic input power;
6. engineering margin.

Actual wire, connector, fuse and PCB copper sizing must follow the measured/declared maximum load.

## Status
These calculations are design checks, not certification. Final thermal/current verification must be done on the assembled hardware.
